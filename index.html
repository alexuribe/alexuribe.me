Hi, I’m Alexandre.

I live in Seattle and work on data and analytics at Amazon Pharmacy. I joined PillPack in 2018, just before the Amazon acquisition closed, and started on the phones with customers.

Before that, I co-founded two startups: one building connected sensors for aquatic ecosystems, and one helping people find a local dentist, with a catalog of procedures and their prices. In between, I ran a design-thinking and rapid-prototyping program at Miami Dade College. Both startups were attempts at zero to one; PillPack was my first look at one to n.

When I’m not working, I like tinkering with technology and playing outside.
